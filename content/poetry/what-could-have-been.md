## What Could Have Been  
  
It's strange how people come and go, And leave us missing them more than they know. A few little moments, a smile or two, And all the things we hoped would come true.  
  
Some days I wish I'd never tried, But I remember how happy I was inside. Back when a little hope was enough, Before it all became this tough.  
  
I'm not angry, just a little sad, Missing something I never really had. And though there's nothing left to say, Some thoughts just never go away.  
  
I never wanted much, you see, Just someone who'd want to be with me. A walk, a laugh, a place to sit, Ordinary days with love in them a bit.  
  
Maybe someday I won't feel this way, And these thoughts will slowly fade away. But for now, every now and then, I still think of what could have been.
